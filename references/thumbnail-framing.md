# Thumbnail profile framing

Use this house layout for future Siljangnim covers, based on the approved Euler, IK and Ray Marching profile-cover revisions of 2026-10-30. Preserve the inspected reference’s typography and artwork treatment while making the complete title-and-hero composition compact and centered.

## Layout

- Deliver a separate 1080×1920 portrait cover. Keep essential title and artwork together near the canvas center, with generous empty space above and below. Do not place a large headline near the top or stretch the hero edge to edge.
- Start with essential content inside x=14%..86%, y=28%..73% (approximately x151..929, y538..1402 at 1080×1920). These are composition targets, not a requirement to fill the box. Adjust optical balance against the approved reference.
- Treat title and hero as one block. Leave comfortable room between them and around both. Background may bleed beyond the content region.
- Video subtitle anchors and the older 270px/14% vertical padding rule do not define thumbnail safety. The central thumbnail region is intentionally smaller.

## Crop review before delivery

Inspect actual rendered images in all three views, using centered crops without stretching:

| View | Crop on a 1080×1920 image |
|---|---|
| Full portrait 9:16 | x0..1080, y0..1920 |
| Profile preview 3:4 | x0..1080, y240..1680 |
| Square preview 1:1 | x0..1080, y420..1500 |

All essential text and recognizable artwork must survive both crops with comfortable margins. Check readability at small profile-tile size, not just the full-resolution image. If clipped or crowded, shrink and reposition the whole title-and-hero group instead of independently compressing or distorting its pieces. Record the reference and crop previews in production notes.

These centered crops are a house review baseline, not an official guarantee about every Instagram placement. If the actual target profile crop is available, inspect that too; record simulated checks as simulations. Follow an explicit user-selected crop when supplied.

Always inspect an accessible user-approved reference image as required by SKILL.md. If none is available, ask for its location or the image before generating the cover. Do not bundle private episode artwork into the public skill repository merely to serve as a reference.
